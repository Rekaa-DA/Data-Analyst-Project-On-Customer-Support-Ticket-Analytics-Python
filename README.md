# 🎫 Customer Support Ticket Analysis

## 📌 Project Summary

📊 This project analyzes customer support ticket data to understand recurring customer issues, priority distribution and overall service quality.

🎯 The project combines text data cleaning, keyword analysis and priority-level analysis to identify common customer complaints, recurring service issues and areas for operational improvement.

The analysis provides data-driven insights to help organizations improve customer support efficiency, reduce response time and enhance overall customer satisfaction.

## ⚡ Data Cleaning & Text Processing

* Converted text data to lowercase for consistency
* Removed punctuation from ticket descriptions
* Normalized extra spaces
* Cleaned customer feedback text for accurate analysis
* Extracted keywords from issue descriptions
* Analyzed word frequency and unique word patterns
* Categorized tickets based on priority levels

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Analyze customer support ticket priority distribution
2. Identify recurring customer complaints and service issues
3. Analyze common keywords in customer feedback
4. Compare positive and negative feedback patterns
5. Understand the relationship between message length and customer frustration
6. Identify frequently occurring service-related issues
7. Evaluate overall customer support quality
8. Provide data-driven recommendations to improve service performance


## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains structured customer support ticket records.

Each ticket includes information related to:

* 🎫 Ticket Number
* 👤 Customer Name
* 📝 Issue Description
* 🚨 Priority Level

The dataset supports both categorical analysis and text-based analysis of customer feedback.

## 📊 Key Metrics

| Metric                | Analysis                                         |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 🎫 Support Tickets    | Total number of customer support records         |
| 🚨 High Priority      | Highest priority ticket category                 |
| ⚠️ Medium Priority    | Moderate priority support issues                 |
| 📉 Low Priority       | Comparatively fewer low-priority tickets         |
| 📝 Issue Descriptions | Text-based customer feedback                     |

---

## 🚨 Priority Distribution Analysis

High Priority tickets form the majority of the dataset.

Medium Priority tickets appear in moderate numbers, while Low Priority tickets are comparatively fewer.

### 💡 Insight

The high volume of High Priority tickets indicates:

* Increased workload on the customer support team
* Recurring critical service-related problems
* Higher operational pressure
* The need for faster issue resolution

---

## 🔍 Issue Pattern Analysis

Keyword analysis was performed on customer issue descriptions to identify recurring complaint patterns.

### 🔴 Frequently Observed Negative Keywords

* Slow
* Poor
* Bad Handling

### 🟢 Positive Keywords

* Good
* Excellent
* Helpful

### 💡 Insight

Negative feedback is more dominant than positive feedback.

The primary complaint patterns are related to:

* Slow service
* Poor support quality
* Response issues
* Service performance problems

This indicates a need to improve both operational performance and customer support response quality.

## 📝 Customer Feedback Behavior

Customer message length was analyzed to understand feedback complexity.

### Short Messages

Usually represent:

* Simple issues
* Quick complaints
* Specific service problems

### Long Messages

Usually represent:

* Complex problems
* Repeated unresolved issues
* Higher customer frustration
* Need for deeper investigation

### 💡 Insight

Longer customer messages may indicate unresolved or repeated problems and can be used as an additional indicator of customer frustration.

## 🔤 Unique Word Analysis

A wide range of unique words was identified across customer feedback.

Frequently occurring words included:

* Support
* Service
* Issue
* Slow
* Good
* Poor

### 💡 Insight

Customer feedback is primarily focused on:

* Service quality
* Support performance
* Response time
* Recurring operational issues

## 🧠 Key Insights

1. 🚨 High Priority tickets dominate the dataset.
2. 🐌 Slow service is one of the major recurring complaint patterns.
3. 📉 Negative feedback is more frequent than positive feedback.
4. 🔁 Repeated keywords indicate recurring unresolved issues.
5. 📝 Longer messages may indicate increased customer frustration.
6. 🛠️ Service performance issues are a major driver of customer complaints.
7. 👥 High ticket priority indicates operational pressure on the support team.

## 📈 Analytics Approach

### 📊 Descriptive Analysis

Analyzed ticket priority distribution and overall customer feedback patterns.

### 🔍 Diagnostic Analysis

Identified recurring keywords and complaint patterns related to slow service and poor support quality.

### 🧠 Text Analysis

### 💡 Prescriptive Recommendations

Recommended improvements in system performance, support response time, team efficiency and continuous ticket monitoring.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Improve system performance to reduce slow service complaints.

2. Reduce response time for High Priority tickets.

3. Monitor recurring keywords for early issue detection.

4. Improve customer support team efficiency through training and process optimization.

5. Use message length as an additional indicator to identify potentially frustrated customers.

6. Perform regular analysis of ticket trends to identify recurring problems.

7. Prioritize recurring service issues for faster root-cause investigation.

## 📌 Final Conclusion

Customer dissatisfaction is mainly driven by slow service and poor support quality.

The dominance of High Priority tickets indicates urgent customer needs and operational pressure on the support team.

The analysis demonstrates how text cleaning, keyword analysis and priority distribution analysis can help identify recurring customer issues and support business decision-making.

Improving system performance, reducing response time and addressing recurring support issues can help improve customer satisfaction and reduce the overall volume of High Priority tickets.
